# panelSCM 0.1.0

## Initial CRAN Release

* First release of panelSCM
* Implements panel-data extension of Synthetic Control Method
* Estimates heterogeneous treatment effects (HATT)
* Functions:
  - `prep_scm_panel()`: Prepare synthetic control models for panel data
  - `estimate_hatt()`: Estimate heterogeneous average treatment effects
  - `plot_scm_fit()`: Plot donor pool optimization
  - `plot_scm_hatt()`: Visualize treatment effects
* Methodology described in Baumert and Harmening (forthcoming, Quality & Quantity)
